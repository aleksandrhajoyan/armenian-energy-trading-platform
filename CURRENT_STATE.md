- **Project start:** 4 September 2026
- **Planning target for first serious complete end-to-end engineering v1:** approximately 7–21 October 2026 (planning target; subject to revision)
- **Central expectation:** ~4–6 additional weeks of intensive daily development from the 9 September planning point (planning estimate; subject to revision)

**Engineering/platform completion** (planning target: roughly first half / middle of October 2026) means the platform can work end-to-end with synthetic, public, or file-based inputs. That is distinct from **Armenia-specific live trading validation**, which requires authoritative real data and market validation and cannot honestly be dated until those sources are available. Future status wording may be: `Armenia-specific model calibration and live-market validation pending authoritative data access.` Engineering completion does not equal live-market validation.

**Engineering v1** is a planning definition only. It may legitimately include 13 agent boundaries/implementations at required maturity, full orchestration, adapters/ACL, databases, Regulatory RAG architecture, document indexing/search, ML training plus inference framework, synthetic/public-data experimentation, backtesting/simulation, portfolio/risk logic, trading strategy flow, billing/settlement, API surface, tests, observability foundation, failure handling, and production-oriented composition/runtime.

The first 80+ chunks established reusable patterns: ports, canonical DTOs, error contracts, async conventions, composition roots, provider adapters, settings patterns, lifespan patterns, architecture tests, and publication discipline. Some future agents/features may therefore implement faster. Velocity is still expected to vary and will slow in empirical areas such as ML experiments, real datasets, feature engineering, backtesting, market validation, live integrations, edge cases, and production hardening. Do not assume linear acceleration.

Lack of authoritative Armenian market data must not stop unrelated platform engineering. External sources remain replaceable behind the existing ACL:

```text
API / Excel / CSV / manual file / scraper / public dataset / synthetic dataset
        ↓
source-specific Adapter / ACL
        ↓
canonical internal model
        ↓
rest of platform
```

Source schemas must not leak into application, domain, or ML. Three planning data levels (not implemented by this chunk):

1. **Level 1 — Synthetic / fixture data.** Realistic synthetic series can validate ingestion, forecast pipelines, risk, trading, settlement, and end-to-end orchestration, including seasonality, hourly peaks, weather influence, noise, missing intervals, anomalies, and price volatility. Synthetic data proves that the engineering system works. It does **not** prove that the model performs well on the real Armenian market.
2. **Level 2 — Public / proxy datasets.** May validate ML engineering, backtesting infrastructure, and general forecasting behavior. They must not be presented as Armenia-specific model-quality evidence.
3. **Level 3 — File-first future integration.** Later authoritative data may arrive through messy Excel, CSV, API, manual files, or scraped sources and must enter via source-specific adapters/ACL. Unreadable/unmappable inputs follow existing diagnostic/DLQ policy.
